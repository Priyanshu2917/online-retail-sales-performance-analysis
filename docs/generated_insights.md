# Evidence-Based Insights

All figures below use the positive completed-sales fact table produced by `src/analysis.py`.

- Clean sales revenue: 20,476,260.45.
- The peak complete month was 2011-11. The final available month is treated as partial when the source ends before month-end.
- **Protect the core market:** United Kingdom revenue share is 85.0% of clean sales. Track UK repeat demand and service levels because the revenue base is concentrated there.
- **Test the leading international market:** EIRE is the highest-revenue non-UK market at 3.2% of clean sales. Run a small, measurable retention or promotion test before expanding international spend.
- **Use merchandise concentration in planning:** The top 10 merchandise SKUs contribute 7.8% of merchandise revenue. The leading SKU is 22423 (REGENCY CAKESTAND 3 TIER). Review availability and replenishment for high-revenue SKUs, while avoiding decisions based on revenue alone.
- **Target lapsed high-frequency customers:** The RFM At Risk segment represents 8.9% of known-customer revenue. Peak complete-month revenue was 2011-11. Test a win-back offer against a holdout group and measure incremental repeat orders.
